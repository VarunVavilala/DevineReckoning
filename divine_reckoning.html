01 — Project Overview

A world older than memory

Project Concept

An archaeologist vanishes into an ancient Indian ruin. His friend follows — through a primeval forest, behind a waterfall, into a cave, toward a temple older than history.

There, a god reveals that the seals holding back a world-ending force are breaking. The protagonist must master divine arts, gather allies, and stop what is coming. Divine Reckoning is the environment and cinematic world built to tell that journey.

Core Idea

The world is a descent: from living jungle to dark cave to sacred stone. Each space changes light, colour and mood so the viewer feels the story before any dialogue — golden and humid outside, cold and oppressive inside, sacred and charged at the heart.

Project Goals

  1. Immersive Indian-inspired worldA dense forest, a cave and an ancient temple.
  2. Environmental storytellingLight, decay and detail communicate the story.
  3. Unreal Engine 5 workflowsLandscape, materials, foliage, lighting and fog.
  4. VFX & atmosphereVolumetric fog, god rays, drifting particles, fire.
  5. A playable, demo-ready experienceExplorable in VR through a standalone Android build.
  6. A visually convincing final sceneDistinct mood and colour in every zone.

04 — Challenges & Learning

Problems met, lessons kept

Challenge → approach → learning, drawn from what the screenshots show.

Area
Challenge
Approach
Learning
Lighting
Early placeholder light washed the cave out to flat blue-white.
Directional light, height fog, sky atmosphere, sky light and volumetric cloud tuned per level.
Lighting is a storytelling tool, not only realism.
Materials
Rock textures showed an obvious repeating tile pattern on the landscape.
Material functions with tiling and offset inputs to vary the texture.
Visible repetition breaks realism fast.
Foliage
A forest that feels dense yet still reads clearly from the camera.
A dedicated forest map with layered trees, backdrop forest walls and drifting light.
Density needs control, not just quantity.
Cave
Landscape cannot form overhangs, so a cave cannot be sculpted in it.
Model the cave as a separate mesh and place it over the terrain.
Choose the right tool for each form.
VR movement
Moving comfortably through a large space in VR.
A teleport trace set up in Blueprints with a visualizer, driven by an input action.
VR needs its own interaction design.
Delivery
Turning the scene into something the jury can run.
Android packaging configured (APK, minimum SDK 29, target 34) with game data packed inside.
A running build is a different discipline from a good screenshot.
